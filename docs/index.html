Power and cooling constraints represent significant vulnerabilities for AI/Hyperscale facilities. Kinetic vulnerabilities include substations and water supplies, where disruption can halt operations. The "Compute Divide", as noted in the Knowledge Base, exacerbates these vulnerabilities. Specifically, 32 countries (out of ~200 globally) host specialized AI data centers at all, meaning over 80% of nations have zero infrastructure for large-scale AI compute. Locations with planned AI/Hyperscale expansions (e.g., France's Serverfarm AI Data Center, Netherlands' Serverfarm AMS1 Expansion) depend on stable, high-capacity grids. Dependencies on specific suppliers and vendors in countries with lax cybersecurity controls add to the risk. A single point of failure, such as a compromised power substation, could trigger cascading effects that impact multiple data centers and disrupt AI-driven services.
