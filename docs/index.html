Energy & Supply Chain (PIR 3)

Power and cooling constraints represent significant vulnerabilities in the AI and hyperscale data center ecosystem. The "Global Data Center Density and AI Compute Concentration" report notes the increasing power density of new facilities driven by AI workloads. These facilities demand massive power and space, placing strain on energy infrastructure. The planned AWS Susquehanna Campus, powered by nuclear energy, illustrates the reliance on stable and high-capacity power sources. Cooling requirements also pose challenges, particularly in regions with limited water resources. Kinetic vulnerabilities include substations and water supply systems, which could be targeted in physical attacks or disrupted by natural disasters. The 'Compute Divide' exacerbates these issues because countries lacking infrastructure are even more dependent on external resources, and are thus exposed to disruptions in power and networking in other regions.
